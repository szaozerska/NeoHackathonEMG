# Pitch (draft)

It's 9 p.m. in the office. You've been heads-down on your startup all day, and your cofounder is across the room doing the same. What if you could stand up, put on a headset, and throw a fireball at them?

**[Game name]** is a two-player wizard duel in mixed reality. You both see the real office through a Meta Quest 3, and the spells appear in it: a fireball in your palm, a sword in your fist, a shield on your open hand. You dodge behind real desks. Your opponent's real body is the target.

You don't press buttons. You cast with your hands. A Meta Neural Band reads the electrical signals of your forearm muscles, and our models turn them into spells: snap to conjure a fireball, make a fist for a sword, open your palm for a shield.

The part a controller can't give you: **the band feels how hard you're squeezing.** A tighter fist hits harder. A firmly braced palm blocks more. Each player calibrates once, so a light grip and a strong grip play fair, and how much you commit to a spell actually matters.

Both headsets share one coordinate system for the room, and one authoritative server runs the whole fight, so both players always see the same fireball fly at the same spot.

No arena, no controllers, no setup beyond a QR code on the floor. Just the room you're already in, and a friend.
