Comparison protocol

  1. Start a session. Choose a component. Prepare its starting pose while red, then press Space when that component begins.
  2. Cross fingers only; then separately test ring/pinky curl with fingers already crossed; then separately test thumb movement with the preceding pose already prepared.
  3. Repeat each component about five times, including relaxed baselines and undoing motions. Key 4 optionally marks the stable hold after the movement.
  4. Select Full sequence. Hold Space at crossing onset, press 2 at curl onset, 3 at thumb approach onset, then 4 at the final hold. If you miss a phase report, do not invent its timestamp; leave it for review.
  5. Undo the gesture before releasing Space. Stop & save when finished.

Raw EMG, reported intent, selected component, per-step timestamps, clock alignment and transitions are preserved separately. Red includes preparation context and is not automatically certified as a relaxed hand. No classifier or camera supplies truth labels.
