How to record

  1. Start a session and choose one of the four actions.
  2. Hold Space as you begin the action. Make the motion, then bring your fingers back out.
  3. Release Space when done. Choose another action or repeat.
  4. For Each process, perform all three motions in order and return, while holding Space throughout.
  5. Click Stop & save session when finished.

Only your selected action and Space press/release label the recording. Internal step boundaries are left unlabeled for later signal analysis.
