The recording loop

  1. Click Start session. Collect relaxed rest or ordinary movement in red.
  2. Select your gesture, then a strength for Shield or Sword. Press and hold Space to switch green. Intended is active immediately; perform your motion.
  3. When finished, release Space to return to red immediately. Repeat as needed.
  4. Click Stop & save session.

Your selected motion/effort and keyboard reports supply the labels. Camera and model predictions do not supply labels. This session is reserved for evaluation; no fitting or threshold tuning uses it. All reported intervals are retained, including short ones. Space boundaries are participant reports, not verified physical onsets. Keep this data reserved until a model and thresholds are frozen.
