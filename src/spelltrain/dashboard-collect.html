What gets saved and what happens next

Raw EMG, all available band context, independent camera landmarks and video/audio, trial cues, participant-confirmed counts, failures, quality checks, and frozen-model predictions share a monotonic timeline. Camera estimates, cues, and predictions never become verified labels automatically.

Snaps are count-confirmed clips, not invented onset timestamps. Non-use trials label absence of intentional snaps, not a single static hand shape. Stable open Fireball holds are kept distinct from the existing shield class. Transition clips retain their full time series for later boundary review.

No model retrains during recording. After review, fit a separate candidate, choose thresholds and the opening window using validation sessions, then evaluate untouched wearing sessions. Event precision, false triggers per minute, missed snaps, and transition latency need appropriate reviewed labels; counts alone cannot establish event accuracy.
