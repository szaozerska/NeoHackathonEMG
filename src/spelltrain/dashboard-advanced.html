Guided hand-state trial

Annotate actions

Shield: extend arm, open palm outward, fingers up; tension controls strength while held. Sword: close fist at any angle, open to dismiss. Fireball: middle-thumb snap, hold, then throw automatically.

Only verified annotations train models. Use the same trial ID for all stages of one action. A snap is a point event; contact without snapping is a hard negative. Shield tracking must verify fingers up and palm facing the opponent.
