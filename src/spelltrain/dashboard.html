For snaps and comparisons, record first, then perform the motion. “Received” confirms signal capture; “I did it” confirms your intended action.

What this trains

Shield: open hand, fingers up, palm toward the opponent. Keep the pose to hold it; forearm effort controls strength continuously. No thrust or jerk. Quest will track position, orientation and pose validity as you move your arm. Sword: a closed fist at any angle summons it; opening dismisses it. Fireball: a middle-finger/thumb snap creates a held fireball; a later throw releases it in the Quest/Unity flow.

Band-only collection trains muscle signals, not spatial pose. Light / medium / firm are your reported effort levels, not measured force. Exact snap times require later review; no snap point events are invented from recording buttons. Models are not trained automatically.
