The recording loop

  1. Click Start session. Collect relaxed rest or ordinary movement in red.
  2. Select Snap, or a Shield/Sword effort. Press and hold Space to switch green. Wait for Label interval active, then perform your intended motion.
  3. When finished, pause briefly and release Space to return to red immediately. Repeat as needed.
  4. Click Stop & save session.

Your selected motion/effort and keyboard reports supply the labels. No camera validation, model predictions, or snap counts are required. Short sections inside guard windows are excluded, not guessed.
