The recording loop

  1. Click Start session. Collect relaxed rest or ordinary movement in red.
  2. Select Snap, or a Shield/Sword effort. Press and hold Space to switch green. Intended is active immediately; perform your motion.
  3. When finished, release Space to return to red immediately. Repeat as needed.
  4. Click Stop & save session.

Your selected motion/effort and keyboard reports supply the labels. No camera validation, model predictions, or snap counts are required. All reported intervals are retained, including short ones. Actual movement boundaries will be refined in post-processing using the surrounding baseline.
