The recording loop

  1. Choose static or dynamic, then click Start session. Collect baseline movement without the gesture in red.
  2. At your first finger interaction, press and hold Space to switch green. Cross the two fingers, then tap your thumb to your pinky. Keep the entire sequence green, including the transitions.
  3. Maintain the gesture for about 30 seconds, moving and rotating your arm naturally in dynamic mode. Undo the gesture, then release Space to return to red. Repeat as needed.
  4. Click Stop & save session.

Animation Gesture and your Spacebar reports supply the labels. No camera validation, model predictions, or snap counts are required. All reported intervals and transition waveforms are retained, including short ones. Internal finger-step times are not verified labels.
