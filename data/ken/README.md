# data/ken

Ken's recordings, all from one band session (`ken-wearing-01`).

- `guided/`: guided-collector captures (`guided-trials.json` + raw `streams.jsonl`): 20 snaps (with audio
  reference), 3 × 30 s open hand, 3 × 30 s fist and about 5 minutes relaxed, and 4 × 30 s Infinite Void holds (`20261108-0741…0744`).
- `superseded-infinite-void-v1/`: Ken's first 8 crossed-finger holds (index over middle, hand up, no thumb-to-pinky
  tap). They predate the agreed Animation Gesture procedure (cross, then tap thumb to pinky), so they are
  **not used for training**. Kept for audit only.
