# Reserved validation recordings

New combined Static/Dynamic recordings save here. The collector is at
`http://127.0.0.1:8835/validation`; see [the protocol](../../docs/combined-validation.md).

These recordings are **reserved evaluation**, not training or threshold-tuning
data. Every trial keeps its Static/Dynamic condition, selected gesture/strength,
and original intended/non-intended Spacebar labels. Raw transitions are preserved.
Do not score until the model/thresholds are frozen, or report software/template
similarity as verified event accuracy.

Only completed, explicitly authorized sessions should be published here with
their unchanged labels, source checksums and lossless raw-stream compression.
